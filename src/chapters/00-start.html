AI Under the Hood · a course you play

What really happens when you talk to an AI?

Seventeen short chapters and a finale, each built around one live simulation you control: from the moment you press send, through the chips and datacenters that serve the reply, to how the model was trained and how it uses tools. Written for a curious beginner, deep enough for an engineer. About 3 to 4 hours, and every claim is referenced.

A simplified picture, in slow motion, with made-up token numbers. Chapter 1 shows the full machine with realistic timings.

How every chapter works

Each chapter answers one question a person would actually ask, in five short beats. It takes 12 to 18 minutes.

  1. PredictMake a quick guess first. Being wrong is the point: it makes the answer stick.
  2. PlayDrive the simulation. Change one thing, watch what happens. "Show me" gives a guided tour.
  3. NoticeA line under the simulation explains what you just caused, with your own numbers.
  4. ExplainA short explanation for everyone, then "How it works" and "The maths" if you want to go deeper.
  5. RememberOne idea to carry forward, two quick questions, and a recap question at the start of the next chapter.
